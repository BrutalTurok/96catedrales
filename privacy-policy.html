Privacy Policy for “96 Cathedrals Spain”

Last updated: 19 October 2026

“96 Cathedrals Spain” is an Android app that lets you keep a personal record of the cathedrals of Spain you have visited. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it.

Summary

The app has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. Its developer does not receive or store any of your data: there is no server of our own that the app sends information to. Everything you record is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the information you enter in its private storage on your phone: the cathedrals you mark as visited and the date you did so, your ratings, your notes, the achievements you unlock and a copy of the photos you add under “My photos”.

This data is not sent to the developer or to any third party. If Android backup to your Google Account is turned on, the system may include the app's data in that backup; that service is run by Google under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

Permissions the app requests

Location. Optional. It is only requested when you tap the location button on the map. It is used only on the device, to show your position on the map and work out your distance to each cathedral. Your location is not stored or sent anywhere, and the app does not access it while it is closed.

Notifications. Optional. Used to tell you when you unlock an achievement. Notifications are generated on the device itself.

Photos. The app has no access to your photo library. When you add a photo, you choose it with the system photo picker and the app only receives the image you select, a copy of which it keeps in its private storage.

Internet. Used only to download the map, the photos of the cathedrals and the address of their English Wikipedia articles, as explained below.

Connections to third-party services

To show some content, the app connects directly from your device to these services. As with any internet connection, the service receives your IP address and the technical details of the request.

OpenStreetMap. Map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap servers. The service receives which area of the map is requested. Privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. When you open a cathedral's page, its photographs and the title of its English Wikipedia article are retrieved from Wikimedia Foundation servers. The service receives which articles and images are requested. Privacy policy: foundation.wikimedia.org.

The downloaded map and photographs are kept in the device's storage so they can be viewed later without a connection.

External links

Each cathedral's page includes buttons that open pages outside the app in your browser or in other apps: the cathedral's official website, Wikipedia and your maps app. Those sites have their own privacy policies, over which the developer has no control.

How to delete your data

You can delete all saved information at any time from Android settings, under Apps ▸ 96 Cathedrals Spain ▸ Storage ▸ Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. As the developer keeps none of your data, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and does not collect personal data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the new date shown at the top.
